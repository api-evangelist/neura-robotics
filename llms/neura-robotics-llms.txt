# NEURA Robotics

> NEURA Robotics is a German deep-tech company building cognitive and humanoid robots for industry, business, and everyday life. It develops robots such as the 4NE-1 humanoid and the MAiRA, MiPA, LARA, and MAV cognitive cobots, powered by its proprietary AI system AURA. The Neuraverse ecosystem connects robots, developers, and partners so AI models, applications, and hardware come together. As of this profile the company exposes no public, documented developer API — neuraverse.com is a gated single-page application portal and no OpenAPI, developer documentation, or well-known discovery endpoints are published.
